ABOUT

Long-term ownership. Thoughtful stewardship.

Granite Commonwealth is a New England-based holding company focused on acquiring, investing in, and growing exceptional businesses.

We believe the best companies are built on strong relationships, enduring reputations, and a commitment to doing things well. Our approach is intentionally long term: we seek opportunities where we can be a responsible steward of an established business and help create its next chapter.
